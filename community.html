Support

Getting help and reporting problems

Reporting a bug

Please include:

  • Your TORQUE version (currently v2.5) and Windows build
  • GPU model and driver version
  • The Activity Log export (Settings → About → Activity log)
  • What you expected and what happened instead

Security concerns

If you have a concern about the download, a detection on VirusTotal, or the behavior of TORQUE, report it through the support channel with the SHA-256 of your file and a link to the scan. We take these reports seriously and respond publicly where appropriate.

Community guidelines

Keeping the community useful

  • Be respectful and keep discussion on topic.
  • Back performance claims with benchmark data where possible.
  • Do not share cracked, modified or re-hosted copies of TORQUE. Always download from this site.
  • Never share passwords or personal information.
