FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find it? Ask in the Discord.

General
Is TORQUE free?

Yes. TORQUE Free covers LOW to MAX, Game Boost, Benchmark Lab, Health check, game configs and every safety feature. TORQUE Plus is an optional paid build with ULTRA and extra tools.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and administrator rights. Driver tuning is NVIDIA-only for now; on AMD, Windows, game and network tuning all work.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Plus uses a key from the Discord instead.

Performance
How much FPS will I gain?

It depends on your PC and game. The biggest gains usually come from Health check items (refresh rate, RAM at its rated speed, Resizable BAR) and from game settings. Windows tweaks mostly make frame times steadier rather than raising average FPS. Benchmark before and after to see your own numbers.

What do Proven, Situational and Experimental mean?

How strong the evidence is. Proven tweaks are documented and measured. Situational ones help on some PCs. Experimental ones are unproven, are never applied automatically below ULTRA, and should be benchmarked.

Does it work on old PCs or in a VM?

Yes. TORQUE switches to a low-performance interface automatically in VMs, remote desktop and on PCs without full GPU rendering.

Safety
Can it break my PC?

TORQUE only changes documented settings, previews each one, makes a restore point and backs up original values. Failed changes roll back automatically and you can revert everything at any time. Use ULTRA with care.

Can I get banned?

TORQUE doesn't inject into games, read their memory or run while they start. It changes Windows and driver settings, and game settings files while games are closed. The crosshair overlay is a separate window; check your game's rules for overlays.

How do I undo everything?

Backups → Revert everything, then restart. Or revert any single change from the Tweaks or Backups page.

Plus
How do I get Plus?

Buy it in the Discord shop. A mod confirms your payment, you get the Plus role and a key, then download Plus with /download.

Will Free keep getting updates?

Yes. Free and Plus are released together.

I updated and a tweak I used is now Plus. What happens?

It stays applied, and you can still revert it from Backups in Free. Only re-applying it needs Plus.
