Privacy & terms

Plain-language privacy.

The app

  • No account and no telemetry. The only optional usage data is an anonymous live-user counter (a random id and the version), off by default.
  • Network use: the DNS benchmark and ping test when you run them, links you open, and one small signed update file from this website (can be switched off).
  • Plus licensing sends your key and a one-way hash of your PC id to the TORQUE license server when you activate and about once a day after. Nothing else.
  • Settings, backups, the activity log and benchmark runs stay on your PC in %ProgramData%\Torque.
  • Exported profiles and share codes contain look and optimization settings only, never passwords, backups or file paths.

This website

No analytics, ads or cookies. Fonts are self-hosted, so your browser talks only to GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data under GitHub's privacy policy.

Discord and purchases

Purchases happen in the TORQUE Discord. The bot stores your Discord user id, your order (product, length, amount, payment method you chose) and your key's status so it can deliver and renew Plus. Discord's own terms and privacy policy apply to the server.

Terms

  • As is. TORQUE is provided without warranty. It changes system settings; changes are previewed, backed up and reversible, but you use it at your own risk.
  • Games. Each publisher sets its own rules. You're responsible for using TORQUE within the rules of the games and tournaments you play.
  • Plus keys are for your own PCs (up to 2). Sharing, reselling or cracking keys or the Plus build gets the key revoked.
  • Distribution. Download TORQUE only from this site or the TORQUE Discord. Don't redistribute modified copies.
  • TORQUE isn't affiliated with Microsoft, NVIDIA, AMD, Epic Games, Riot Games or any other company.
