Privacy and terms of use

A plain-language summary of how TORQUE and this website handle data, and the terms under which the software is provided.

Privacy

The TORQUE application

  • TORQUE does not require an account and does not collect telemetry. The only optional usage data is an anonymous live-user ping (a random id and the version number), which is off by default and can be enabled in Settings.
  • Its network activity is the optional DNS benchmark and ping test, links that you choose to open, and a small read of latest.json from this website to show “update available” (switch it off in Settings). Nothing is downloaded or installed automatically.
  • Configuration, backups, the activity log, benchmark results and Fortnite settings backups are stored locally on your PC in %ProgramData%\Torque\.
  • Exported .torque profiles contain appearance and optimization settings only. They do not include passwords, backups or file paths.

This website

  • The site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when you view a page.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • If you join our Discord server, Discord's terms and privacy policy apply to that service.
