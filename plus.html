Plus FAQ

Good to know.

What happens when my 30 days end?

Plus features lock again and you get a DM from the bot. Tweaks you already applied stay applied and can still be reverted, because revert is free forever.

Can I use my key on more than one PC?

Yes, on up to 2 PCs. If you replace a PC, ask a mod in a ticket to reset your devices.

Does Plus work offline?

Yes. The app re-checks your key about once a day and keeps working for 7 days without internet.

Why is Plus a separate download?

The Free download contains no Plus code at all, so there is nothing to unlock or crack. The Plus build only runs Plus features with an active key, and the Plus tweaks are sent to your PC only after the key is checked.

Refunds?

Open a ticket in the Discord before you buy if you're unsure Plus will help your PC. Refund requests are handled case by case in your ticket.
